## Comments

### Threads and authorship

View anchored comment threads, and create, edit, reply to, and delete comments.
Each comment records its author, which decides who may edit or delete it.

### Resolving and reopening

Resolving a thread hides its highlight; reopening puts it back.

### Deleting commented text

Deleting or cutting commented text never deletes the comment.
Trimming the text keeps the comment on what remains; deleting all of it lists the comment under
All comments as *Original content deleted*.
In the saved file it is an ordinary comment at the place the text was.

### The comment body

A comment body keeps its formatting through an edit; the built-in composer writes plain text.

## Footnotes and endnotes

A footnote is drawn at the foot of the page that holds its reference, under a separator line and
above the footer, and endnotes are listed with their formatting after the last page.
What a note is drawn without is listed under
[Pages and sections](/docs/features/pages-and-sections#limitations).
With page guides turned off, footnotes are listed there too, ahead of the endnotes.
Notes are numbered in the document's own format, starting number, and section restarts; a restart
on every page is counted straight through.
### Editing a footnote

Click a footnote's number, or the footnote itself at the foot of the page, to put the caret in it;
`Escape` puts the caret back just after the number, and clicking the document leaves as well.
Insert a footnote at the caret with **Insert footnote** on the right-click menu over the text, or
with `Mod+Alt+F`; either puts the caret in the new footnote.

A footnote takes character and paragraph formatting: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color,
highlight, font, size, paragraph style, alignment, spacing, indentation, and tabs.
Tables, images, links, comments, lists, page breaks, and further footnotes cannot be added inside
one, and a paste into a footnote drops images, links, and footnote references; whatever a
document's own footnote already holds is kept and its text stays editable.
Footnotes are edited on the page, so with page guides turned off the listed footnotes are read-only.

Everything typed in a footnote goes into the document's own history, so one `Mod+Z` takes it back
from anywhere.
Deleting a footnote reference deletes its footnote, and undo brings both back; `Backspace` at the
start of a footnote holding nothing but its number deletes the footnote and its number and returns
the caret to where the number stood.
A footnote left empty is kept.

Notes cannot be edited in comment-only or read-only mode, where the footnote a reader opens takes no
typing, and endnotes cannot be edited in any mode.
A selection that runs across an endnote reference cannot be replaced, so typing or deleting over it
leaves the text as it stands.

[Comments and notes](/docs/editor-api/comments-and-notes) in the Editor API lists the commands and
queries behind these controls.
