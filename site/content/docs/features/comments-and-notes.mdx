## Comments

### Threads and authorship

View anchored comment threads, and create, edit, reply to, and delete comments.
Each comment records its author, which decides who may edit or delete it.

### Resolving and reopening

Resolving a thread hides its highlight; reopening puts it back.

### Deleting commented text

Deleting or cutting commented text never deletes the comment.
Trimming the text keeps the comment on what remains; deleting all of it lists the comment under
All comments as *Original content deleted*.
In the saved file it is an ordinary comment at the place the text was.

### The comment body

A comment body keeps its formatting through an edit; the built-in composer writes plain text.

## Footnotes and endnotes

A footnote is drawn at the foot of the page that holds its reference, under a separator line and
above the footer, and the endnotes are drawn after the last paragraph of the document, under a
separator line of their own, onto further pages where they do not fit.
What a note is drawn without is listed under
[Pages and sections](/docs/features/pages-and-sections#limitations).
With page guides turned off, both are listed under the document instead, the footnotes ahead of the
endnotes.
Notes are numbered in the document's own format, starting number, and section restarts; a restart
on every page is counted straight through.

### Editing a note

Click a note's number, or the note itself where it is drawn, to put the caret in it.
An endnote stands at the end of the document, so clicking its number is also what takes a reader
there.
Clicking the number a note opens with puts the caret back just after the reference that calls it
and scrolls there; `Escape` does the same, and clicking the document leaves as well.
Insert a note at the caret with **Insert footnote** and **Insert endnote** on the right-click menu,
over body text and inside a table cell alike, or with `Mod+Alt+F` and `Mod+Alt+D` (`Mod+Alt+E` on
macOS, which keeps Command+Option+D for itself); either puts the caret in the new note.

A note takes character and paragraph formatting: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color,
highlight, font, size, paragraph style, alignment, spacing, indentation, and tabs.
Tables, images, links, comments, lists, page breaks, and further notes cannot be added inside one,
and a paste into a note drops images, links, and note references; whatever a document's own note
already holds is kept and its text stays editable.
Notes are edited where they are drawn on the page, so with page guides turned off the listed notes
are read-only.

Everything typed in a note goes into the document's own history, so one `Mod+Z` takes it back from
anywhere.
Deleting a note's reference deletes the note, and undo brings both back; `Backspace` at the start of
a note holding nothing but its number deletes the note and its number and returns the caret to where
the number stood.
Selecting a note's text and deleting it leaves the number standing, since the number is the note's
own rather than text written in it, and one undo brings the text back with it.
A note left empty is kept.

Notes cannot be edited in comment-only or read-only mode, where the note a reader opens takes no
typing.

A document that asks for its endnotes at the end of each section gets them at the end of the
document.

[Comments and notes](/docs/editor-api/comments-and-notes) in the Editor API lists the commands and
queries behind these controls.
