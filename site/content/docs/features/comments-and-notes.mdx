## Comments

### Threads and authorship

View anchored comment threads, and create, edit, reply to, and delete comments.
Each comment records its author, which decides who may edit or delete it.

### Resolving and reopening

Resolving a thread hides its highlight; reopening puts it back.

### Deleting commented text

Deleting or cutting commented text never deletes the comment.
Trimming the text keeps the comment on what remains; deleting all of it lists the comment under
All comments as *Original content deleted*.
In the saved file it is an ordinary comment at the place the text was.

### The comment body

A comment body keeps its formatting through an edit; the built-in composer writes plain text.

## Footnotes and endnotes

A footnote is drawn at the foot of the page that holds its reference, under a separator line and
above the footer; endnotes are drawn after the last paragraph of the document, under a separator
line of their own, running onto further pages where they do not fit.
With page guides turned off, both are listed under the document instead, footnotes first.
Notes are numbered in the document's own format, starting number, and section restarts.

### Editing a note

Click a note's number, or the note itself, to put the caret in it; for an endnote, clicking the
number is also what takes you to the end of the document.
`Escape` or a click on the number the note opens with puts the caret back just after the reference
and scrolls there, and a click on the document leaves the note as well.
Insert a note at the caret with **Insert footnote** and **Insert endnote** on the right-click menu,
in body text or in a table cell, or with `Mod+Alt+F` and `Mod+Alt+D` (`Mod+Alt+E` on macOS); the
caret moves into the new note.

A note takes character and paragraph formatting: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color,
highlight, font, size, paragraph style, alignment, spacing, indentation, and tabs.
Tables, images, links, comments, lists, page breaks, and further notes cannot be added inside one,
and a paste into a note drops images, links, and note references; whatever a document's own note
already holds is kept and its text stays editable.

Everything typed in a note goes into the document's history, so `Mod+Z` takes it back from
anywhere.
Deleting a note's reference deletes the note, and undo brings both back; `Backspace` at the start
of a note holding nothing but its number does the same and returns the caret to where the
reference stood.
Deleting a note's text leaves its number standing, and a note left empty is kept.

### Limitations

Notes cannot be edited in comment-only or read-only mode, and the notes listed with page guides
turned off are read-only.

A numbering restart on every page is counted straight through, and a document that asks for its
endnotes at the end of each section gets them at the end of the document.

[Pages and sections](/docs/features/pages-and-sections#limitations) lists what a note is drawn
without on the page.

[Comments and notes](/docs/editor-api/comments-and-notes) in the Editor API lists the commands and
queries behind these controls.
