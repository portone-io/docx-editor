---
title: Preserved content
description: What the editor keeps untouched, and how it behaves while you edit around it.
---

## How preservation works

Untouched package parts and document blocks go back out unchanged, and an element the editor has
no model for is kept where it stood while the paragraph around it stays editable.
An unsupported block, or a table whose structure cannot be modelled, stands as a placeholder.
A section break is kept as well; [Pages and sections](/docs/features/pages-and-sections#sections)
covers what it means while you edit.

## Character formatting and styles

Character formatting and character styles the editor does not display or edit are preserved.

## Bookmarks and ranges

Bookmark ranges, permission ranges, and the markers of a moved passage are kept at their positions,
invisible and impossible to remove.
A selection that runs across one of the markers cannot be replaced, so typing or deleting over it
leaves the text as it stands; move the selection clear of the marker to edit around it.
Markers between a table's rows or cells also decide which
[table edits](/docs/features/tables#limitations) are available.

## Fields

A complex field shows its markers and instructions as small boxes that cannot be edited or removed;
its cached result stays editable text.
A simple field is one box showing its cached result and can be removed whole.
Nothing recalculates a field, so a page number or a total stays as it was written.

## Tracked changes

A tracked insertion or deletion is shown as a small box carrying its text, which can be selected
and removed whole but not edited inside.
A table's `w:tblGridChange` is preserved as it came.

## Content controls

Edit or lock supported text and table-cell content controls; a control nested inside another
control or a hyperlink is editable text and goes back out nested as it came.
A control the editor could not take apart is shown as a box.
[Locked content](/docs/editor-api/modes-and-locks#locked-content) covers locking a control.

## Other markup

A symbol, a positional tab, and any other element without a model are shown as small boxes where
they stood.
`documentFidelity` and `importDocx` list what a document kept this way; see
[what a document loses](/docs/core#what-a-document-loses).
