---
title: Preserved content
description: What the editor keeps untouched, and how it behaves while you edit around it.
---

## How preservation works

Content outside the supported editing surface is not silently discarded.
Untouched package parts and document blocks are written back from their original XML, and an
element the editor has no model for is kept where it stood: inside its run, beside the runs of its
paragraph, or as a block.

A paragraph stays editable around the content preserved inside it, and a table stays a table around
a bookmark that spans one of its columns.

Unsupported blocks stand as placeholders, as do tables whose structure cannot be modelled,
including a table with a content control or a tracked change around a whole row.

A section break is kept as well; [Pages and sections](/docs/features/pages-and-sections#sections)
covers what it means while you edit.

## Character formatting and styles

Character formatting and character styles that the editor does not display or edit are preserved.

## Bookmarks and ranges

Bookmark ranges are kept at their document positions, invisible and impossible to remove.

A selection running across one of the markers cannot be replaced, so typing or deleting over it
leaves the text as it stands, and the picture, line break, and page break controls are unavailable
while it is held.
Move the selection clear of the marker to edit the text around it.

The same holds for a permission range and for the markers of a moved passage.

Markers carried between a table's rows or cells also shape which
[table edits](/docs/features/tables#limitations) are available.

## Fields

The markers and instructions of a complex field are shown as small boxes that cannot be edited or
removed, and its cached result stays editable text.
A simple field is one box showing its cached result, and it can be removed whole.
The paragraph around either kind stays editable.

Nothing recalculates a field.
A page number or a total the document already drew stays as it was written, even after you edit
the cached result.

## Tracked changes

A tracked insertion or deletion is shown as a small box carrying its text.
The box can be selected and removed whole, but the passage inside it cannot be edited.

A table's `w:tblGridChange` is preserved as it came.
A rebuilt table is the exception in one place: the cells continuing a vertical merge are written
from the cell that starts the merge, so properties of their own do not come along.

## Content controls

Edit or lock supported text and table-cell content controls.
A control standing inside another control or inside a hyperlink, at any depth, is editable text
like any other, and goes back out nested as it came.
A control the editor could not take apart is shown as a box.

[Locked content](/docs/editor-api/modes-and-locks#locked-content) covers locking a control.

## Other markup

A symbol, a positional tab, and any other element the editor has no model for are shown as small
boxes where they stood, and the paragraph around them stays editable.

`documentFidelity` lists what a given document kept this way, and `importDocx` returns the same
list for a file opened outside the editor, described in
[what a document loses](/docs/core#what-a-document-loses).
